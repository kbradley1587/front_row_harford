Oscar Wilde's *The Importance of Being Earnest* is no small feat of a play, set in the late-1800s Victorian era — but the students at Patterson Mill Middle/High School, under the direction of Caitlyn Shaffstall and student direction of Hope Boyle, did a marvelous job bringing this quick-witted, silly comedy to life!

I, myself, got the chance to play Algernon Moncrieff in *The Importance of Being Earnest* when I was a senior at Patterson Mill, and went through the show experience on that same stage twelve years ago. Overridden with nostalgia for my own high school experience, and combined with a very high-quality production of this Wilde play, many kudos go to the cast and crew of *Earnest*. Lots of "Bunburying," convincing British accents, physical comedy, and strong facial expressions — especially from Raegan Decker as Gwendolen and Gianna Libertini as Lady Bracknell in this production.

It is wonderful to see high school students involved in the performing arts, especially knowing how dedicated and knowledgeable Ms. Shaffstall is with the craft. You can tell, just from being in the presence of the students after the performance, how much Ms. Shaffstall means to them, and what she brings to the youth theatre opportunities here in Harford County and beyond.
